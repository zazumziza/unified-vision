# Decisions

1. BT is not a Spotify clone.
2. One unified fan/artist experience; contextual artist controls.
3. Person/Profile and ArtistIdentity are distinct.
4. BAD TUNE 🔥 is positive.
5. One BT membership is the subscription abstraction.
6. Payment processing remains provider/rail-bound; BT is not modeled as a bank.
7. Source observations are not automatically canonical truth.
8. Uncertain entity matches are not silently merged.
9. The client does not mutate financial accounting records.
10. Lovable builds experience on this foundation rather than inventing a parallel model.
